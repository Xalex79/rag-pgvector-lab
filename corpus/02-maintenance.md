# Maintenance

Regular maintenance keeps seal quality stable and prevents unplanned stops. Always switch off the main
isolator and close the compressed air valve before any maintenance task, unless the task says otherwise.

## Daily checks

At the start of every shift the operator must check the following:

- The sealing plate is clean and free of film residue. Wipe it with a dry cloth while it is cold.
- The silicone sealing rubber in the lower tool has no cuts or flattened areas.
- The film roll is centred and the film runs without wrinkles.
- The air pressure gauge reads between 5.5 and 7 bar.
- The emergency stop button stops the machine when pressed (test it once per shift).

## Weekly tasks

Once a week, clean the tray tooling with warm water and a food-safe detergent, then dry it completely.
Drain the water separator of the air filter regulator by pressing the drain valve at the bottom of the
bowl. Check that the scrap film rewinder pulls the waste film with even tension.

## Replacing the sealing rubber

Replace the silicone sealing rubber every 250,000 cycles, or earlier if it shows cuts or permanent
marks. The cycle counter is under Service > Counters on the touch panel.

1. Let the sealing plate cool below 40 °C. The panel shows the current temperature.
2. Remove the lower tool by releasing the two quick-release clamps.
3. Pull the old rubber out of its groove and clean the groove with isopropyl alcohol.
4. Press the new rubber into the groove, starting at the corners. Do not stretch it.
5. Refit the lower tool and reset the rubber counter under Service > Counters.

## Lubrication

The linear guides of the lifting unit need food-grade grease (NSF H1) every 500 operating hours. Apply
two strokes of the grease gun to each of the four grease nipples. Do not lubricate the sealing area.

## Spare parts

Keep at least one sealing rubber, one set of cutting knives and one heating element in stock. Order
spare parts using the part numbers shown in the spare parts list under Service > Parts on the touch
panel.
