# Safety

Read this chapter before operating or servicing the VX-40. Only trained people may operate the machine.

## Hot surfaces

The sealing plate works at up to 220 °C and stays hot for more than 30 minutes after the machine is
switched off. Wear heat-resistant gloves for any task near the sealing area and wait until the panel
shows a plate temperature below 40 °C before touching it.

## Guards and interlocks

All guards have safety interlock switches. Opening a guard stops all movements and switches off the
heating. Never bypass or bridge an interlock switch. Damaged guards or interlocks must be repaired
before the machine is used again.

## Lockout and tagout

Before maintenance, switch off the main isolator, lock it with your personal padlock and attach a tag
with your name. Close the compressed air valve and release the residual pressure with the exhaust valve
on the filter regulator. Each person working on the machine uses their own padlock.

## Emergency stop

The red emergency stop buttons on the operator panel and at the back of the machine stop all
movements immediately and release the air pressure from the cylinders. To restart, remove the cause,
release the button by turning it clockwise and press the reset button on the panel.

## Personal protective equipment

Operators must wear safety shoes and, in the sealing area, heat-resistant gloves. Hearing protection is
not required: the noise level at the operator position is below 70 dB(A).
