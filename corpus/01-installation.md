# Installation

This manual covers the Acme VX-40 tray sealer, a fictional semi-automatic machine that seals pre-filled
food trays with a heat-sealed lidding film. It was written for this project as a sample corpus.

## Site requirements

Install the VX-40 on a level concrete floor that can carry at least 400 kg. Leave a clearance of 80 cm
on the operator side and 50 cm on the other three sides for maintenance access. The room temperature
must stay between 5 °C and 35 °C, with relative humidity below 80 % and no condensation.

## Electrical connection

The machine needs a three-phase 400 V, 50 Hz supply with neutral and protective earth, protected by a
16 A type C circuit breaker and a 30 mA residual current device. Total power consumption is 3.2 kW.
Only a qualified electrician may connect the supply cable to the main terminal block inside the
electrical cabinet. Check the direction of rotation of the film unwind motor after connecting: if it
turns backwards, swap two phases.

## Compressed air

The pneumatic system needs clean, dry compressed air at 6 bar (minimum 5.5 bar, maximum 7 bar) with a
flow of at least 120 litres per minute. Connect the supply to the 8 mm push-in fitting on the filter
regulator at the back of the machine. Set the regulator to 6 bar and confirm the reading on the gauge.

## First start-up

1. Check that the emergency stop button is released and that all guards are closed.
2. Switch on the main isolator. The touch panel starts in about 40 seconds.
3. Log in with the commissioning account and load the default recipe for 1-compartment trays.
4. Let the sealing plate reach its set temperature (default 160 °C). This takes about 12 minutes.
5. Run three empty cycles before sealing any product.
