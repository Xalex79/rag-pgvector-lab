# Troubleshooting

Alarms appear on the touch panel with a code and a short text. Acknowledge the alarm only after you
have removed its cause. The alarm history is stored under Service > Alarm log.

## Weak or leaking seals

If trays leak at the seal, check these causes in order:

1. Sealing temperature too low for the film. Raise it in steps of 5 °C, up to the film supplier's limit.
2. Sealing time too short. The default is 1.2 seconds; try 1.5 seconds for thick films.
3. Product or liquid on the tray flange. Improve filling so the flange stays clean.
4. Worn or damaged sealing rubber. See "Replacing the sealing rubber" in the maintenance chapter.

## Wrinkles in the film

Wrinkles usually come from wrong film tension or a film roll that is not centred. Check that the roll
sits against both centring cones and that the brake of the unwind shaft is set to the value in the
recipe. A damaged film core can also cause wrinkles; replace the roll if the core is crushed.

## Alarm E12: sealing temperature not reached

The sealing plate did not reach the set temperature within 20 minutes. Check that the thermocouple
connector is fully inserted. Measure the resistance of the heating element: it should be between 38
and 42 ohms when cold. If it is outside that range, replace the heating element.

## Alarm E21: low air pressure

The pressure switch detected less than 5 bar. Check the compressed air supply and the setting of the
filter regulator. If the pressure drops only during the sealing stroke, look for leaks in the hoses of
the lifting cylinder.

## Alarm E35: film break

The film sensor no longer detects film. Thread the film again following the threading diagram on the
inside of the front guard, then restart the cycle from the panel.
